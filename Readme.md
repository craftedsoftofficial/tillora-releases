# Tillora by CraftedSoft

Billing that sells for you: fast, GST-ready billing, stock and customer management for retail shops. Works fully offline on Windows.

## Download

**[Download the latest Tillora for Windows](../../releases/latest/download/Tillora-Setup.exe)**

Every version is listed under [Releases](../../releases), with what changed.

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 4 GB RAM or more
- 14-day free trial with every feature; no card, no sign-up
- Your data stays on your own PC

## Installing

1. Download `Tillora-Setup.exe` and run it.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Follow the two-minute setup: pick your shop type, add your shop details and owner PIN.

To update, install the new version over the old one. Your bills, products and settings are kept.
